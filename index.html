Workflow

1

Extract the game

Dialogue (DLG), creatures (CRE) and strings are dumped from the game files into a dialog report. Every line gets a speaker, its text and its original sound file, if it has one. Lines the game already voiced keep their original audio.

2

Plan & split

Each line is split into spoken speech and narration. Narration goes to a single storyteller voice. Pronunciation rules fix invented words and names.

3

Cast the voices

Companions and key characters are cloned from their original acting. Everyone else is cast by an LLM (Qwen, local) from a character profile (age, personality, voice description) onto a library voice from Neverwinter Nights or World of Warcraft.

4

Add voice direction (Qwen)

A local Qwen3.6 35B-A3B reads the line, its context and the speaker, then writes a short acting direction such as [mocking, sweetly sinister] and marks emphasis. It may not change the words: the result is checked against the original text.

5

Generate with Fish Drama 3

Drama 3 speaks the directed text in the cloned voice. Bad takes (cut off, too short, stalled) are rolled again with a new seed. The work is spread across several PCs with their own GPUs.

6

Review & build the mod

Takes are stitched, silence-trimmed and reviewed, then packaged as a WeiDU mod that replaces the game's voice files.

Fish Drama 3 setup

Model & runtime

  • Drama 3 final weights, Q4_K GGUF (Drama-35B-A3B-v3), community quant by tunjayoff
  • Runtime: speech.cpp 0.10.1 (Windows, Vulkan), one persistent speech worker per GPU
  • Cloning: multi voice mode, one voice per character built from ~10–30 s of reference clips with transcripts
  • Direction: a leading [free-form direction], <emphasis> tags and pronunciation fixes, written per line by a local LLM director (Strata, Qwen3.6 35B-A3B Q4_K_M)
  • Narration and directed takes are generated separately and stitched; narration is left out of this page

Sampling parameters

top_k4097
top_p0.9
temperature1.0
seedper segment, reproducible (shown under Take details)
max_seconds3 s + 0.7 s per word (runaway-silence guard)
retriesup to 3 takes; truncated, too-short or stalled takes are re-rolled

A small top-k makes Drama 3 stall in silence at sentence ends, hence 4097. Samples here all passed on the first attempt.
